    How it works

    What happens when a woman taps your link

    1. She opens the check from InstagramEach link you share carries its own tag (bio, story, partner), so you can see what works. Tested inside Instagram’s own browser.
    2. She answers eight questionsHer result is worked out on her phone. Only anonymous counts are sent, so no tracking cookies and no cookie banner. She can skip the personal questions.
    3. She sees her result straight awayScored the same way every time, with one next step and a gentle care note when she is low. She can also send one small ask to someone she trusts, in one tap; nothing is sent for her.
    4. She chooses to joinEmail notes, and, separately and optionally, keeping her wellbeing answers. GDPR treats those answers as health data, so that consent stands on its own.
    5. Stored in the EUA secure database in the EU, holding only what she agreed to.
    6. She confirms, then gets your welcome emailDouble opt-in, sent from your own domain with authentication, so it lands in the inbox.
    7. You see it on your dashboardResults, seasons, sources and sign-ups, with export and deletion on request.

    Built to be safe from day one

    • Wellness wording only, never diagnosis
    • Separate, explicit consent for wellbeing answers
    • Data kept in the EU, only what is needed
    • Privacy notice, consent records and an 18+ confirmation
    • Link previews for Instagram and WhatsApp
    • Fonts hosted with the site, nothing sent to third parties
    • Spam and bot protection on the form

    Checked before launch

    • Every result path tested, including ties
    • iPhone, Android and the Instagram in-app browser
    • Large text, dark mode and screen readers
    • Emails tested in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail
    • You approve it live before anything is invoiced
